About Elongation at Break

Elongation at break measures the maximum strain (percentage stretch) a polymer can undergo before rupture. It indicates the ductility and flexibility of a material, with higher values representing more stretchable polymers. This property is crucial for applications requiring materials that can withstand significant deformation.

Dataset Details

Source: PUE643 dataset - 643 polyurethane elastomers with mechanical property measurements
Reference: Ding et al. (2021) J Appl Polym Sci, DOI: 10.1002/app.51269
Samples: 514 training, 129 test
Representation: 20 molecular descriptors (no SMILES available)
Units: % (Percentage)

Limitations & Future Work

The absence of SMILES data limits this benchmark to descriptor-based models. Graph neural networks (GNN, MPNN) cannot be evaluated without molecular structure information. Future work may include SMILES reconstruction through literature matching, as 99.8% of samples have unique property combinations that could enable identification.
