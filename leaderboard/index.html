About

This leaderboard tracks the state-of-the-art in machine learning models for predicting polymer properties. Models are evaluated on standardized datasets and ranked by RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error) - lower is better.

Dataset: Models marked "Verified" are trained on the TgSS dataset (7,284 polymers with glass transition temperatures). Other models are from the POINT2 benchmark paper.

Contribute: Want to add your model to the leaderboard? Open an issue or pull request on GitHub.
