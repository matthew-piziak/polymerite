About Polymerite

Polymerite is a benchmark for machine learning models that predict polymer properties. Models are evaluated on standardized datasets and ranked by RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error) - lower is better.

Heterogeneous Benchmark: Different properties have different data availability. This reflects the real landscape of polymer informatics and demonstrates the practical limitations researchers face when working with polymer data.

Why Data Type Matters: SMILES representations enable graph neural networks (GNN, MPNN), while descriptor-only datasets limit models to traditional ML approaches (MLP, Random Forest). The mechanical properties currently lack SMILES data, presenting an opportunity for future data enrichment.
